## How the values are measured

- **Tip deflection** is u_y at the reference node, read from the `.dat` file (`*NODE PRINT`), because ccx does not write the reference node to the `.frd`. The web-centreline tip node (from the `.frd`) is shown alongside as a check on the coupling.
- **Midspan σ_xx** is the nodal (extrapolated) stress from the `.frd`, averaged over all nodes on the outer flange surface at x = L/2 across the full flange width, as the spec's Further Notes allow.
- **Signs.** The −y tip load gives a hogging moment along the cantilever, so the top flange is in tension (+) and the bottom flange in compression (−). Spec #1 says the top flange is compressive. That is a sign slip in the spec; the values here are as computed.
- **Root peak von Mises** is the largest nodal von Mises stress on the root face. It is for information only, because the fixed end disturbs it (St-Venant). Beam theory gives P·L·c/I = 162.55 MPa there. It grows with refinement, as expected at the stress singularity where the fixed face meets the free surfaces.
- **Spec figures.** Spec #1 quotes I ≈ 1.943×10⁷ mm⁴, δ_EB ≈ 22.06 mm and a shear contribution of about 5 %. Those belong to the rolled section with fillet radii. The no-fillet section modelled here gives the values above, and shear adds about 1.5 %.
