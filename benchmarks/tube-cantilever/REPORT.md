## Verdict

**PASS**. The rule: PASS when the fine mesh level has |error vs δ_T| ≤ 1 % and |midspan stress fit error| ≤ 1 % at both fibres, **and** the midspan stress fit error magnitude strictly decreases coarse → medium → fine at both fibres. The deflection is not part of the shrink condition, because it sits on its modelling floor from the medium level on. The single-node stresses, the information-only deflections and the root peak stress do not affect it. Without all three levels there is no verdict.

## How the values are measured

- **Tip deflection** is u_y at the reference node, read from the `.dat` file (`*NODE PRINT`), because ccx does not write the reference node to the `.frd`. The tip-face mean deflection (mean u_y of all tip-face nodes, from the `.frd`) is shown alongside as a check on the coupling; the tube has no material at its centroid, so there is no centreline node to compare with.
- **Deflection floor.** From the medium level on, the deflection error sits at about −0.007 % against δ_T. That is a modelling residual, the difference between the clamped solid loaded through a distributing coupling and Timoshenko beam theory, at the resolution of the `.dat` print. It is not mesh error, so the deflection's observed order is not defined.
- **Midspan stress fit** is the least-squares fit σ_xx = a·y through the origin over every outer-surface node at x = L/2 (corner and midside), using the nodal (extrapolated) σ_xx from the `.frd`. It gives +a·r_o at the top fibre and −a·r_o at the bottom fibre.
- **Single-node stresses** are the nodal σ_xx at the top-fibre (y = r_o) and bottom-fibre (y = −r_o) nodes at x = L/2, shown next to the fit for comparison.
- **Signs.** The −y tip load gives a hogging moment along the cantilever, so the top fibre is in tension (+) and the bottom fibre in compression (−). Stress errors keep their sign, so a flipped sign shows as about −200 % and fails.
- **Section discretisation error.** The targets use the exact annulus, while the mesh approximates the circle with quadratic arcs, so the stress error falls as the circumferential divisions grow.
- **Shear.** With Cowper's κ, shear adds 4.7 % to the Euler-Bernoulli deflection. The errors against Euler-Bernoulli and against the thin-wall κ = 0.5 are shown for information, to show that the FE result tells them apart.
- **Root peak von Mises** is the largest nodal von Mises stress on the root face. It is for information only, because the fixed end disturbs it (St-Venant). Beam theory gives P·L·r_o/I = 64.87 MPa there.
- **Observed order** p = ln((e_c − e_m)/(e_m − e_f)) / ln 2 from the signed errors of the three levels, for information. It is n/a when the ratio is not positive or a level is missing.
