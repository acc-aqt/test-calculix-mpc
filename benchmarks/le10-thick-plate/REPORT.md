## How the values are measured

- **σ_yy(D)** is ccx's nodal σ_yy at point D, read from the `.frd`: ccx extrapolates the integration-point stresses of each element to its nodes and averages them over the elements that share the node. At D only one element does.
- **Integration point** is the integration point of that element nearest D, printed to the `.dat` with `*EL PRINT`. It shows how much of the nodal value comes from extrapolation.
- **C3D20** (information) solves the same deck with full integration in place of C3D20R's reduced integration. It shows how sensitive σ_yy(D) is to the integration rule and never counts towards the verdict.
